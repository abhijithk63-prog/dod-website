Common Questions
We've Got
Answers.
Do I need to provide my own car?
For the Driver on Demand service, we drive your car — you don't need to arrange a vehicle. For driving lessons, we use your own car so you learn in the vehicle you'll actually be driving day to day.
Are drivers available at night and on weekends?
Yes, DoD operates 24/7 including weekends and public holidays. Night drivers are specifically available from 8 PM to 6 AM. Simply book in advance or request on-demand.
How is the driver verified and background checked?
Every DoD driver goes through a thorough onboarding process — government ID verification, police background check, driving licence validation, and a test drive before they join the platform. You can see their profile and ratings before they arrive.
What is the minimum booking duration?
The minimum booking for our Driver on Demand service is 2 hours. For driving lessons, each session is 1 hour. There's no maximum — you can book for a full day or multi-day outstation trips at a daily rate.
How do I pay? Is cash accepted?
We accept UPI, bank transfer, and cash. Payment is made after the trip or session is completed. You'll receive a clear invoice with a breakdown — no surprise charges.
Can I cancel or reschedule my booking?
Yes. Cancellations made at least 1 hour before the booking are free of charge. Rescheduling can be done anytime by messaging us on WhatsApp — we'll match you with the next available driver or instructor.
Do you offer outstation and long-distance trips?
Yes. We handle outstation trips from HSR Layout and Bommanahalli to anywhere in Karnataka and beyond. Outstation bookings are priced separately — WhatsApp us with your destination and dates for a custom quote.
Is DoD available for corporate bulk bookings?
Absolutely. We work with companies that need regular drivers for staff travel, executive pickups, or client management. Monthly packages with dedicated drivers are available. Reach out via WhatsApp to discuss your requirements.
